About The Club

Open to All Majors. No Experience Needed.

The MIS Club at UMass Dartmouth is a student organization within Charlton College of Business dedicated to bridging the gap between business strategy and information technology.

Whether you are a business major curious about tech, a CS student interested in the corporate side, or any student who wants to develop professional skills — you belong here.
