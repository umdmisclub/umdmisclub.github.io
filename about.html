Who We Are

The MIS Club is a student-led organization founded in Spring 2024 by Management Information Systems (MIS) students with interests spanning business technology and digital transformation. Open to all majors, the club welcomes anyone who wants to explore how MIS tools and strategies can strengthen real-world business decisions and support operations across industries.


Our mission is to help members build practical, career-ready skills and feel confident entering the business world—whether you're pursuing MIS or simply want to gain in-demand knowledge that complements your major. Through a mix of activities, workshops, and networking gatherings, members have opportunities to learn, collaborate, and explore emerging trends while working toward their academic and professional goals.


Led by students and supported by professors across disciplines, the MIS Club hosts events featuring career development, industry tools and platforms, AI trends and applications, live demos, and other topics relevant to today's workplace. Each session is designed as a practical mini-workshop, offering actionable insights, resources, and hands-on guidance that show how MIS connects to—and adds value within—many fields.
